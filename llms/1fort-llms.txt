# 1Fort AI

> 1Fort AI is a New York–based platform that uses AI to automate workflows for commercial insurance brokers and agencies. It autofills applications, retrieves quotes, compares options, processes binds and payments, and handles ongoing policy servicing — collapsing manual, email-and-PDF-heavy back-office work into a fast automated workflow, so brokers spend less time on paperwork and more time with clients.

1Fort is a technology platform for brokers, not an insurance seller. Brokers use it as an "autopilot" for their submissions and servicing across their own markets. The platform serves brokers and agencies across the United States.
